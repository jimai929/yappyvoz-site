Download YappyVoz

Install it yourself in a few minutes. When you open it, a guide walks you through each step.

Android (phone)

Download for Android

Version 0.1.0 · Size 3.3 MB
SHA-256: 5b09843d99343835ac7737dfd2d39f3f3c8510a74d9dbd94813ce0658567077f

  1. Tap Download for Android. If the browser warns the file may be harmful, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file (at the bottom of the browser, or in the Downloads folder).
  3. The phone asks whether to allow installing apps from the browser. Pick your brand:
    Honor / Huawei
    1. “For your security, your phone is not allowed to install apps from this source” appears. Tap Settings.
    2. Turn on Allow app installs and go back.
    3. If “External app installation” appears, tick that you understand the risk and tap Continue.
    Samsung
    1. Tap Settings in the notice and turn on Allow from this source.
    2. If Auto Blocker stops it: Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → off, then try again.
    Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO
    1. Tap Settings in the notice and turn on Allow from this source.
    2. MIUI shows a warning with a 10-second countdown: wait and tap OK.
    Motorola
    1. Tap Settings in the notice and turn on Allow from this source; go back.
    Others
    1. Tap Settings in the notice and turn on Allow from this source (or “Install unknown apps”); go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.
  5. Open YappyVoz Gmail and follow the guide: every screen has a button that opens the setting to change.
